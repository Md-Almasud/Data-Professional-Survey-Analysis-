**Data Professional Survey Analysis**

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing survey responses from data professionals to explore career preferences, salaries, demographics, programming languages, and job satisfaction.

**Project Overview**

This project explores insights from a survey of 630 data professionals using Microsoft Power BI. The objective is to understand the current landscape of the data industry, including salary trends, career transitions, preferred programming languages, and workplace satisfaction.

**Tools & Technologies**

Microsoft Power BI – Data visualization and interactive dashboard development

Microsoft Excel – Data preparation and initial data analysis

**Dataset Overview**

The dataset contains 630 survey responses covering various aspects of data professionals, including:

Job titles and industries

Salary information

Favorite programming languages

Career transitions into data

Gender and age demographics

Geographic distribution

Work-life balance and salary satisfaction

Factors influencing job changes

**Key Analysis Areas**

1. Career Insights

Percentage of respondents who switched careers into data.

Perceived difficulty of entering the data industry.

2. Salary Analysis

Average salary comparison across different data job titles.

3. Programming Language Preferences

Most popular programming languages among data professionals.

4. Demographic Analysis

Distribution of respondents by gender and continent.

5. Job Preferences

Most important factors when looking for a new job, including salary, remote work, work-life balance, and company culture.

**Key Takeaways**

Python is the most popular programming language among survey respondents.

Better salary is the leading factor influencing respondents when considering a new job.

The average work-life balance rating is 5.73 out of 10.

Average salary satisfaction stands at 4.28 out of 10.

The survey includes professionals from multiple continents and job roles.
